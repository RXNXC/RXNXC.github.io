PROCESS

From question
to clarity

A focused analytical workflow for turning vague business problems into sharp, decision-ready findings.

PHASE 01

Define

Clarify the business question, identify the relevant data, and set the analytical frame before opening a single file.

PHASE 02

Clean

Audit every column for nulls, duplicates, and type mismatches — then treat each with explicit, documented decisions.

PHASE 03

Analyze

Select metrics that directly answer the question. Use rates over raw counts, segment by what matters, build structures that hold scrutiny.

PHASE 04

Communicate

Package findings into dashboards and documents that give stakeholders a clear action, not just a chart to stare at.
